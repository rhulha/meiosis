Meiosis Steps

MEIOSIS I (Reductive Division)
1. Prophase I    - Chromosomes condense; homologs pair up (synapsis); crossing over occurs between non-sister chromatids
2. Metaphase I   - Homologous pairs align at the cell equator (metaphase plate)
3. Anaphase I    - Homologous pairs pulled apart to opposite poles (sister chromatids stay together)
4. Telophase I   - Two haploid cells form, each with duplicated chromosomes

MEIOSIS II (Equational Division)
5. Prophase II   - Chromosomes condense again in both cells
6. Metaphase II  - Individual chromosomes align at the metaphase plate
7. Anaphase II   - Sister chromatids pulled apart to opposite poles
8. Telophase II  - Four haploid daughter cells (gametes) form, each with unique genetic combinations
